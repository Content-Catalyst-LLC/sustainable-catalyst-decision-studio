Decision workflow

From an open question to a reviewable decision

The Studio is organized around a five-stage workflow that keeps reasoning explicit while allowing deeper analysis to move through the rest of Sustainable Catalyst.

01 · Frame

Define the decision

State the question, objective, constraints, stakeholders, alternatives, criteria, and assumptions before analysis begins.

02 · Evidence

Build the evidence base

Link sources, observations, provenance, evidence quality, unresolved gaps, and the claims they support or challenge.

03 · Analyze

Model tradeoffs and uncertainty

Compare criteria, scenarios, models, sensitivities, risks, uncertainty, and sustainability dimensions without hiding assumptions.

04 · Decide

Form a recommendation

Record the recommendation, confidence, rationale, counterarguments, tradeoffs, and the conditions that would change the decision.

05 · Review

Reassess outcomes

Preserve the decision record, monitor outcomes, revisit assumptions, and document whether the reasoning still holds as evidence changes.

Unified Decision Object · v2.1

One structured object for the entire decision

Decision Studio v2.1 introduces a unified Decision Object so the core components of a decision remain connected instead of being scattered across isolated forms, notes, or exports.

Framing

Question, objective, alternatives, criteria

Keep the decision itself stable while alternatives, constraints, criteria, and stakeholder perspectives evolve.

Reasoning

Evidence, assumptions, models, scenarios

Connect what is known, what is assumed, how analysis was performed, and which scenarios were considered.

Judgment

Tradeoffs, recommendation, confidence

Preserve the logic behind the recommendation, including uncertainty, competing arguments, unresolved issues, and confidence.

Accountability

Provenance, decision, rationale, outcome review

Maintain a traceable record of where inputs came from, what was decided, why it was decided, and what happened afterward.

Evidence & Source Bundles · v2.2

Keep claims connected to reusable source identities

Source Bundles preserve citations, provenance, review state, quality and freshness metadata, while Evidence Bundles link claims to those sources and expose citation gaps, unresolved source relationships, review gaps, and visible support/challenge contradictions.

Sources

Reusable source records

Stable source IDs, citations, provenance, quality, freshness, review state, and SHA-256 fingerprints.

Evidence

Claim-to-source relationships

Evidence records connect claims to one or more sources without flattening the underlying source context.

Coverage

Visible evidence gaps

Citation coverage, unresolved sources, review status, and contradictions remain explicit for human review.

Boundary

No automatic truth claims

Evidence organization improves traceability; it does not certify truth, sufficiency, or professional approval.

Analysis lenses

Sustainability is one structured lens inside a larger decision system

Environmental, social, economic, and governance analysis remains important, but it is no longer the entire definition of Decision Studio. The four pillars can be applied alongside financial, technical, scientific, operational, geographic, and institutional criteria.

Environmental

Ecological and resource effects

Emissions, energy, water, materials, ecosystems, resilience, adaptation, and pollution risk.

Social

People and communities

Stakeholders, equity, access, labor, safety, participation, displacement, and public value.

Economic

Costs, benefits, and feasibility

Capital, operating cost, affordability, ROI, payback, incentives, financing, and opportunity cost.

Governance

Accountability and implementation

Ownership, evidence quality, transparency, review, institutional capacity, compliance, and auditability.

Decision capabilities

What the Studio is built to support

The goal is not to produce a single automatic answer. It is to improve the quality, transparency, and continuity of decision reasoning.

Alternatives

Structured comparison

Compare options against explicit criteria, constraints, evidence, and tradeoffs.

Scenarios

Stress-test choices

Review how alternatives behave under different assumptions, conditions, and future states.

Uncertainty

Confidence and sensitivity

Make uncertainty visible instead of allowing precise-looking outputs to imply false certainty.

Challenge

Counterarguments and review

Record competing interpretations, contradictions, objections, and conditions that would change the recommendation.

Provenance

Traceable reasoning

Keep evidence, source records, assumptions, models, and analytical handoffs attributable.

Outcomes

Decision lifecycle

Preserve the decision after approval so assumptions and results can be reassessed over time.

Decision Studio and Workbench

Choose the tool based on the job

Decision Studio structures a choice. Workbench performs broader computation and technical analysis. They are designed to exchange work rather than duplicate each other.

Use Decision Studio when

You need to structure and defend a decision

  • You are comparing alternatives.
  • You need explicit criteria, assumptions, evidence, and tradeoffs.
  • You want scenarios and uncertainty tied to a recommendation.
  • You need provenance and a reviewable rationale.
  • You want to revisit the decision after outcomes are known.
Use Decision Studio →

Use Workbench when

You need computation or technical analysis

  • You need calculators, equations, units, or engineering logic.
  • You want numerical models and technical outputs.
  • You need graphs or computational validation.
  • You are exploring a technical problem before it becomes a decision.
  • You want results that can be handed back into Decision Studio.

Technical architecture

WordPress interface, Contabo-hosted FastAPI backend, connected platform services

Decision Studio combines a WordPress-integrated interface with a Python/FastAPI backend on the Sustainable Catalyst Contabo infrastructure. The backend exposes decision-object, platform-context, packet, scenario, governance, export, and integration services while preserving deterministic operation when AI assistance is unavailable.

Interface

WordPress

The public page embeds Decision Studio through its shortcode while keeping explanation, boundaries, and workflow close to the tool.

Backend

Python / FastAPI

Decision logic, structured objects, validation, transformations, integrations, and export-ready services run in the backend.

Runtime

Contabo / Docker

The production service runs as a containerized Sustainable Catalyst backend behind the public API gateway.

AI assistance

Optional, bounded support

AI can assist with interpretation and briefing where configured, but structured reasoning and human judgment remain primary.

Boundaries

Analytical decision support, not professional advice

Outputs depend on user inputs, source quality, assumptions, model quality, backend availability, analytical choices, and human review. A structured decision record can improve transparency, but it cannot eliminate uncertainty or transfer accountability to software.

Use responsibly: Do not enter confidential, proprietary, sensitive personal, legal, medical, financial, or regulated information into the public Studio. Use its outputs as structured analytical records, not as certifications, assurance opinions, regulatory approvals, investment recommendations, or substitutes for qualified professional judgment.
