# Decision Studio v2.5.0 — Scenario Comparison & Stress Testing

Release date: 2026-09-11

Adds first-class scenario sets, cross-scenario tradeoff comparison, score ranges, ordering-change and threshold-breach diagnostics, explicit stress-test suites, and Decision Object/Packet attachment. Preserves v2.4.0 uncertainty/confidence and Energy Runtime Consumer v2.3.0.

Human-control boundaries remain unchanged: no scenario-likelihood inference, no approval from a stress-test pass, no automatic winner selection, and no automatic recommendation.
